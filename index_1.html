Le Royal Kraft started operations in 2003 in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, and has grown into a leading manufacturer, supplier, trader and exporter of premium marble statues and handicrafts.

Every piece — from Marble Durga and Ganesh statues to custom bust sculptures and temple handicrafts — is shaped by skilled craftsmen using the finest marble sourced from certified vendors, then finished with advanced tools to meet global quality standards.
